# Glossary

| **_TEA_** (EN)                | **_TEA_** (PT)                  | **_Description_** (EN)                                                                                                                                                                             |                                       
|:------------------------------|:--------------------------------|:---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Carriage**                  | **Carruagem**                   | Part of the train responsible for carrying cargo. Varies depending on the type of cargo it transports and are free of costs of purchase.                                                           |
| **Cargo**                     | **Cargo**                       | List of things carried by the trains, from resources to final products.                                                                                                                            |
| **City**                      | **Cidade**                      | Landmark formed by a gathering of blocks that serve as houses. In this context, works as a consumer and/or a generator.                                                                            |
| **Demand**                    | **Procura**                     | Business received by industries or cities depending on the radius of the associated station.                                                                                                       |
| **"Depot"**                   | **"Armazém"**                   | Stations with fifty thousand capacity and a three by three radius.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Editor**                    | **Editor**                      | User who is able to create maps and scenario from an included bundle or an exclusively sold one.                                                                                                   |
| **Final product**             | **Produto final**               | Range of products, such as food, textiles or cars, made in industries that ended up being consumed by cities.                                                                                      |
| **Historical Restriction**    | **Restrição Histórica**         | Restrictions related to the historical events which may influence the simulation.                                                                                                                  |
| **Industry**                  | **Indústria**                   | Landmarks where products are made or transformed, available in three different types.                                                                                                              |
| **Locomotive**                | **Locomotiva**                  | Part of the train that provides traction to the train. It can run by steam, diesel or electricity and is distinguished by its power, acceleration, top speed, release year of operation and price. |
| **Mail**                      | **Correio**                     | One of the different things consumed and generated by cities.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Mixed industry**            | **Indústria Mista**             | Ports where cargo is either imported or exported.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Passenger**                 | **Passageiro**                  | One of the various things consumed and generated by cities.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Player**                    | **Jogador**                     | User who is only able to play the game, whether single or multiplayer.                                                                                                                             |
| **Primary sector industry**   | **Indústria de setor primário** | Industries that generate either mineral or farming resources.                                                                                                                                      |
| **Product**                   | **Produto**                     | Come from Transforming industries and can be used again to make final products in a different industry.                                                                                            |
| **Railway**                   | **Ferrovia**                    | The metal tracks on which trains run.                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Railway Line**              | **Linha ferroviária**           | Path that connects to stations, with different lengths, sizes and electrification.                                                                                                                 |
| **Resource**                  | **Recurso**                     | Generated in Primary sector industries, end up being consumed or processed in other industries.                                                                                                    |
| **Route**                     | **Rota**                        | List of stations passed by the train, previously determined by the user, and are tracked at the start and in the end.                                                                              |
| **Scenario Editor**           | **Editor de cenário**           | Tool that enables the user to apply restrictions or conditions for using a map.                                                                                                                    |
| **Simulator**                 | **Simulador**                   | Tool that enables the user creation of the railway, which includes stations connected to cities and industries.                                                                                    |
| **Station**                   | **Estação**                     | Landmarks that serve cities and industries connected by lines, where trains collect and deliver their assigned cargo. Can appear in three different types.                                         |
| **"Station"**                 | **"Estação"**                   | Stations with one hundred thousand capacity and a four by four radius.                                                                                                                             |
| **Station upgrade**           | **Melhoria de estação**         | Modification resultant from different buildings, such as telegraphs/telephones, cafés, customs, post offices, hotels, silos or liquid storages, for a better station perfomance.                   |
| **Supply**                    | **Abastecimento**               | Business sent by industries or cities depending on the radius of the associated station.                                                                                                           |
| **System User**               | **Utilizador do sistema**       | Type of user operating the system, either Editor or Player.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Technological Restriction** | **Restrição Tecnológica**       | Restrictions related to the machinery (industries and locomotives) available.                                                                                                                      |
| **"Terminal"**                | **"Terminal"**                  | Stations with two hundred thousand capacity and a five by five radius.                                                                                                                             |
| **Time Restriction**          | **Restrição de Tempo**          | Restrictions related to the time period where the simulation will take place.                                                                                                                      |
| **Train**                     | **Comboio**                     | Machine composed by locomotives and carriages assigned to the transportation of cargo from station to station, following a user implemented route.                                                 |
| **Transforming industry**     | **Indústria Transformadora**    | Industries, such as bakeries, textiles, automobiles or steel mills, that transform the resources into new products.                                                                                |
